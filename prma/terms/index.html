1. What Prma is

Prma helps painters record and learn from their process:

  • Studio: create paintings and add stages. Each stage holds a photo, the six main colors Prma reads from it (HEX and percentage), a label, a note of up to 280 characters and an optional voice note of up to 60 seconds.
  • Tools: Grid, Values, Mix, Compare, Process sheet and 2048px stage export, plus a Paint box of up to 24 paints you name yourself.
  • Community: a feed of painting processes where members post the stages of a painting, comment, like, save, follow, send direct messages and tip other painters with credits.

Values, Mix and Compare are estimates drawn from your photo, its lighting and your own paint colors. Mix and Compare always show: Estimates only. Real pigments mix differently, so test on scrap paper first. Prma does not guarantee that any color, mix or comparison will match real pigments, paper or canvas.
