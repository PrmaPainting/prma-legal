Prma Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 App: Prma (Bundle ID com.prma.apxmhu) Contact: PrmaPainting@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Prma handles, why, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Prma app for iPhone and iPad. Please read it together with the Prma Terms of Use.

1. Summary

  • Prma records each stage of a painting with a photo, its six main colors, a label, a note and an optional voice note, and offers studio tools (Grid, Values, Mix, Compare, Process sheet, Export) and a painters' Community.
  • Prma does not ask you for an email address, phone number, password or real name to use the app.
  • Prma does not show third-party ads, does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell personal information.
  • Photos are read by Prma's own color-reading code inside the app. Prma does not send your photos, notes or recordings to any third-party AI service.
  • You can delete individual items at any time, or everything at once with Settings → Clear All Data.

Where it is kept. Prma stores this information in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad (app files, the app database and app preferences). It stays there until you delete it, use Clear All Data, or delete the app.

Content you publish. When you publish a post, Prma shows the selected stage photos, colors, labels, notes, caption, tags and medium in the Community feed and on your profile in the app. Only publish what you are comfortable sharing.

4. Facial data

This chapter explains how Prma handles photos that may show a person's face. It is written so it can be cited as Prma's complete explanation for App Store Review Guidelines 2.1 and 5.1.1.

4.1 What is collected

Prma does not collect facial data. Prma does not use the TrueDepth camera, Face ID, ARKit face tracking, Vision face detection or any face recognition, face landmark or facial geometry processing, and it does not create faceprints or any other biometric identifiers. A photo you choose to take or pick for a stage, or a picture shown with a Community post, may happen to include a person's face (for example, a painter standing next to their work). Prma treats such a photo exactly like any other photo.

4.2 How photos that may show a face are used

Such photos are used only to: (a) read the six main colors of the image, (b) show the photo in your painting, stage, Process sheet, export or Community post, and (c) run the studio tool you choose (Grid, Values, Mix, Compare) on that photo. Prma does not analyse faces, identify people, infer age, gender, emotion or any other attribute, or use the image for advertising or profiling.

4.3 Sharing and disclosure

Prma does not send these photos to third parties, including AI or analytics providers. A photo is shown in Community only when you choose to publish the stage it belongs to. A photo is saved to your Photos library only when you choose Export or Process sheet.

4.4 Where it is stored and how long it is kept

Stage photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are kept until you delete the stage, the painting or the post, use Settings → Clear All Data, or delete the app. Deleting a stage or painting removes its photos from Prma immediately.

4.5 Scope of this chapter

Sections 4.1 to 4.4 are Prma's full explanation of the collection, use, disclosure, sharing and retention of any image that may contain a face. Prma collects no other facial data.

5. Third-party AI and analysis

Prma does not use third-party AI services. Color reading, the Values, Mix and Compare tools, the Process sheet and exports are produced by Prma's own code inside the app. No prompt, photo, note, recording or other user content is sent to an outside AI provider.

6. In-app purchases and credits

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Prma never receives or stores your payment card details, Apple ID password or billing address. Prma receives Apple's confirmation of the purchase and adds the credits to your balance on this device. Prma also adds 20 free welcome credits the first time you tap Start, and records them in the same history. Prma keeps a history of your purchases, welcome credits, credit spending and tips so it can show your balance and the Tips screen. See the Terms of Use for the credit packs and rules.

7. Community safety information

  • Filtering. Before a post, comment, message, profile, painting title, stage label, note or paint name is saved, Prma checks the text against a list of abusive, hateful, sexual and spam terms, including common character swaps. Text that matches is not published.
  • Report. When you report a post, comment, member or conversation, Prma hides that content from you immediately, blocks its author on your device and creates a report ticket. Prma then offers to open an email draft to PrmaPainting@gmail.com containing the ticket number, content type, content ID, the author's handle, the reason you chose, your optional note and the time of the report. That email is sent only if you send it from your mail app; it then reaches the Prma team, who review reports within 24 hours.
  • Block and remove. Blocking hides a member's posts, comments and messages from you. Remove from feed hides a single post. You can unblock members in Me → Blocked members.
  • Age. Community is for people 18 and older.

8. How to delete your information

  • Single items: delete a stage, a painting, a post, a comment or a paint from its menu. Deleting a painting also removes its stages, photos, notes, voice notes and tool results.
  • Everything: Me → Settings → Clear All Data, then type DELETE to confirm. This deletes every painting, stage photo, note, voice note, draft, paint, tool result, export record, your credit balance and history, your profile, your Community posts, comments, messages, likes, saves, follows, blocks, hidden items and report tickets, your Community Terms agreement and your session. The built-in Community content that ships with the app is shown again as on a fresh install.
  • End Session (Me → Settings → End Session) only returns you to the start screen. It does not delete your data.
  • Deleting the app removes all of Prma's data from the device. Credits cannot be recovered after deletion.

9. Children

Prma is intended for people 18 and older and is not directed at children. Prma does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used Prma, contact us at PrmaPainting@gmail.com.

10. Your choices and rights

You can review, change and delete the information described here directly in the app at any time, and you can withdraw any device permission in iOS Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information; contact us and we will help.

We may update this policy when Prma changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected on this page before they take effect.
