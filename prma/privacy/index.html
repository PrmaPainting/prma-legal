Prma Privacy Policy

Version of November 12, 2026
Covers Prma for iPhone and iPad, bundle com.prma.apxmhu
Write to PrmaPainting@gmail.com

Prma is a process journal for painters. You photograph a canvas at each stage, Prma reads the colors in that photo, and you decide whether to keep the stage to yourself or post it for other painters in Community. This page walks through the material that passes through Prma, what each piece is for, and how to remove it. The rules for using the app are in the Terms of Use.

P1. In brief

  • After Start you choose how to use Community: Sign in with Apple, an account made with a username and a password, or browsing as a guest. Prma never asks for your email address or phone number.
  • Community runs on Prma's server. When Community first opens, Prma gives this install a random member ID; signing in links that ID to your username or Apple ID. What you share in Community is stored on the server so other painters can see it. Your studio (stages you haven't posted, voice notes, paints, tool results and credits) stays on your device.
  • Colors are read from your photos by code built into Prma. Your pictures, notes and voice recordings are never passed to an outside machine-learning company.
  • Prma carries no advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking kits, and nothing you put in Prma is sold.
  • Anything you add can be removed on its own, and Delete Account (Clear All Data for guests) removes all of it in one step.

P2. What you put into Prma

Paintings and stages. A painting has a title and, if you pick one, a medium. Each stage you add to it holds a photo (from the camera or your library), the six dominant colors Prma reads from that photo as HEX values with their share of the image, a short label, a written note of up to 280 characters, and, if you want one, a spoken note of up to 60 seconds. A stage you begin but don't finish is kept as a draft so you can pick it up later.

Your paints and tool results. The Paint box holds up to 24 paints that you name and color yourself; the Mix tool builds its suggestions only from those paints. When you pay credits for a tool (Values levels, Mix, Compare check, Process sheet or the 2048px export), the result is kept so reopening it for the same photo costs nothing.

Credits. Prma keeps your current credit balance and a running history of every credit added or spent: packs bought, the 20 welcome credits added when you create your account, tools paid for, and tips sent or received.

Your account. If you use a username account: your username and your password. The password is stored on Prma's server only as a one-way hash, so nobody, the Prma team included, can read it, and because Prma has no email address for you it can't be recovered or reset. If you sign in with Apple: the user identifier Apple gives Prma and, the first time only, the name you let Apple share, which Prma uses as your Community name if you haven't set one yet. Prma doesn't ask Apple for your email address. Prma also keeps a token from Apple so it can revoke Sign in with Apple for Prma when you delete your account. Prma never sees your Apple ID password. A guest has no username or Apple ID on file.

Your Community presence. The random member ID Prma creates for this install (it is kept when a guest signs in, so saves and blocks carry over); the name (up to 30 characters) you choose when you create a username account, or that Apple shares the first time you use Sign in with Apple, which you can change at any time in Me → Settings → Edit Profile; the bio (up to 120 characters) you set there; the profile photo you choose, if any, when you create an account or later in Me → Settings → Edit Profile (Prma crops it to a square of 512 pixels and stores it on the server); and your handle (your username, or for Sign in with Apple one made from your name); the posts, comments and direct messages you write; the chat requests you send and answer, with the note of up to 150 characters you add to one, if any; the photo you add to a comment or a direct message, if any; the voice messages of up to 60 seconds you record in direct messages; the posts you like and save; the painters you follow or block; the tips you send; the posts and comments you remove from your feed; your report tickets; and the date you accepted the Community Terms.

App state. Whether you have tapped Start, whether you have finished the introduction screens and whether you chose to browse as a guest, so Prma opens on the right screen; and your sign-in session, kept in the iOS Keychain.

P3. Where it lives and who can see it

On your device. Paintings and stages you have not posted, voice notes, drafts, paints, tool results, credits and credit history, the posts and comments you remove from your feed, your report tickets, your Community Terms acceptance and app state are written to Prma's own protected storage on your iPhone or iPad. Other apps cannot read that storage. It stays there until you remove it (see P9).

On Prma's Community server. Community only works if other painters can see what you share, so these items are stored on Prma's server: your member ID and, once you sign in, your username or Apple user identifier; your name, handle and bio; the posts you publish (a copy of each posted photo, whether a stage photo or a picture you pick from your photo library while writing the post, reduced to at most 1600 pixels on its longer side, with its colors and, for a stage, its label and note; the video you add to a post, if any, which you pick from your photo library, up to 60 seconds long and converted on your device to an MP4 of at most 1280 × 720 pixels, together with a still frame from it and that frame's colors; plus the caption, tags and medium); your chat requests (who asked whom, the optional note, when it was sent and whether it was accepted or declined); your comments and direct messages, with the photo you add to one, reduced to at most 1600 pixels on its longer side, and the voice messages you record in direct messages (AAC audio, up to 60 seconds); the posts you like and save; the painters you follow and block; the tips you send; and the reports you file. The server is run for us by Supabase, Inc., a hosting provider, in its data center in Sydney, Australia. Supabase stores and delivers this data on our instructions only. Everything travels between Prma and the server over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

A stage stays private to you until you post it. Posting a stage to Community puts its photo, colors, label, note, caption, tags and medium on the Community feed and on your profile inside Prma; photos you pick from your library and a video you add are shown the same way, the video with its sound. Post only what you are happy for other painters to see. Members can view posted pictures full screen and save posted pictures and videos to their own Photos library.

P5. Faces in photos

This section is written to be quoted on its own as Prma's answer to App Store Review Guidelines 2.1 and 5.1.1 about facial data.

P5.1 Does Prma collect facial data?

No. Prma has no face detection, face recognition, face landmark or facial geometry code, and it never touches Face ID, the TrueDepth camera, ARKit face tracking or Vision face requests. It creates no faceprint and no other biometric identifier. The only way a face reaches Prma is inside an ordinary photo you choose, such as a stage photo where you stand beside your easel or a picture attached to a Community post. Prma handles that photo the same way as a photo of a still life.

P5.2 What a photo with a face is used for

Three things only: reading the six dominant colors of the picture; showing it where you put it (a stage, a painting, a Process sheet, an export or a Community post); and running the studio tool you open on it (Grid, Values, Mix or Compare). Prma does not look for faces, recognise who anyone is, estimate age, gender, mood or any other trait, and does not use such photos for advertising or profiling.

P5.3 Who else can see it

Other painters see a photo only after you post it in Community, either with the stage it belongs to or picked straight from your photo library while writing a post. When you do, a copy of that photo is stored on Prma's Community server, which Supabase, Inc. runs for us as a hosting provider (see P3); Supabase keeps and delivers the file on our instructions and does not analyse it. No other outside company receives these photos, machine-learning and analytics providers included. A photo or video reaches your Photos library only when you save it yourself: an export, a Process sheet, or a post image or video you choose to save.

P5.4 How long it stays and where

A photo you have not posted stays only in Prma's protected storage on your device, for as long as the stage or painting that holds it exists; deleting that stage or painting removes it straight away. The copy of a posted photo stays on Prma's server until you delete the post, which removes it from the server straight away. Deleting a stage or painting on your device does not remove a post you have already published. Me → Settings → Delete Account (Clear All Data for guests) removes both: first the copies on the server, then everything on your device.

P5.5 Completeness of this section

P5.1 to P5.4 together are the whole of Prma's explanation of how images that may show a face are collected, used, disclosed, shared and retained. Prma holds no facial data beyond what is described here.

P6. Outside machine-learning services

Prma does not use any outside machine-learning service. Color reading, the Values, Mix and Compare tools, the Process sheet and every export are computed by Prma's own code inside the app, and no prompt, photo, note, recording or other material of yours is handed to a machine-learning provider.

P7. Buying credits through Apple

Credit packs are consumable purchases handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Your card number, billing address and Apple ID password stay with Apple; Prma only receives Apple's confirmation that a purchase went through, then adds the credits to your balance. The pack list and credit rules are in Part 2 of the Terms of Use.

P8. Keeping Community safe

  • Text check. Before Prma saves a post, comment, message, profile, painting title, stage label, note or paint name, it compares the text with a list of abusive, hateful, explicit and spam words, including disguised spellings, and Prma's server runs the same check again on Community text. Matching text is not saved or shown.
  • Reporting. Reporting a post, comment, painter or conversation hides it from you on the spot, blocks its author for you, and sends a report ticket to the Prma team's moderation queue on the server: the ticket number, the kind of content, its ID, the author's member ID, the reason you picked, any note you add and the time. Prma also offers an email draft to PrmaPainting@gmail.com if you want to add more; it goes nowhere unless you send it from your mail app. The Prma team looks at every report within 24 hours.
  • Blocking and hiding. A blocked painter's posts, comments and messages disappear for you, and Remove from feed hides a single post. Me → Blocked members lets you undo a block.
  • Guests. A guest can browse, save, report, block and remove posts from their feed. Posting, commenting, messaging, liking, following and tipping need a signed-in account.
  • Age. Community is for adults aged 18 and over.

P9. Removing your information

  • One thing at a time. A stage, painting, post, comment or paint can be deleted from its own menu. Deleting a post removes it and its photos and video from Prma's server, and deleting a comment removes its photo too. Deleting a painting takes its stages, photos, notes, voice notes and tool results off your device; posts you already published stay until you delete them.
  • Everything at once. In Me → Settings → Delete Account (shown as Clear All Data while you browse as a guest), type DELETE to confirm. Prma first has the server delete your account and member ID and everything stored with them: your username or Apple user identifier; your profile and profile photo, your posts and their photos and videos, comments, chat requests and messages with their photos and voice messages, likes, saves, follows, blocks and tips. If you signed in with Apple, Prma also asks Apple to revoke Prma's access to your Apple ID. Reports you filed are kept for moderation, with your member ID removed. This step needs an internet connection; if the server can't be reached, nothing is deleted and Prma asks you to try again. Prma then erases every painting, stage photo, note, voice note, draft, paint, tool result and export record; your credit balance and credit history; your hidden posts and comments and report tickets; your Community Terms acceptance; and your session. Community then looks the way it does on a fresh install.
  • Sign Out (Me → Settings → Sign Out) ends the session on this device and takes you back to the sign-in screen. Nothing is deleted: your account and Community content stay on the server, and your paintings and credits stay on the device. Sign in again with the same username and password, or the same Apple ID, to return.
  • Deleting Prma from your device erases what is on the device, and credits lost this way cannot be brought back. It does not remove your Community content from Prma's server: use Delete Account first, or write to PrmaPainting@gmail.com with your handle and we will delete it within 30 days.

P10. Age limit

Prma is made for adults aged 18 and over and is not aimed at children. Prma does not knowingly gather information from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 is using Prma, please tell us at PrmaPainting@gmail.com.

P11. Your say over your information

Everything described in P2 can be viewed, edited or deleted inside Prma whenever you like (your username or Apple ID link is removed with Delete Account), and any permission can be withdrawn in iOS Settings. Privacy laws in your country or state may give you further rights; write to us and we will help you use them.
