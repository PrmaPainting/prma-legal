Part 1 — Using Prma

1.1 What the app does

Prma is a journal for the life of a painting, from first wash to final glaze.

  • Studio. Make a painting and add stages to it. A stage holds a photo, the six dominant colors Prma reads from it (HEX and share of the image), a label, a written note of up to 280 characters and, if you like, a spoken note of up to 60 seconds.
  • Tools. Grid, Values, Mix, Compare, Process sheet and a 2048px stage export, together with a Paint box of up to 24 paints that you name.
  • Community. A feed of painting processes. Painters post any mix of a painting's stages (marked as a painting in the post), up to 12 photos picked from their library and one video of up to 60 seconds, comment, like, save, follow one another, exchange direct messages and send tips in credits. Posted pictures open full screen and posted pictures and videos can be saved to Photos.

1.2 Adults only

Prma is for people aged 18 and over. Each time you use it, you are telling us you have reached that age.

1.3 Signing in, signing out and erasing

  • Start. Tick the box accepting the Community Terms on the start screen, then tap Start.
  • Sign in or browse. Next, choose Continue with Apple (Sign in with Apple), Continue with Email (sign in with your email address and password; when you create the account, Prma emails a 6-digit code once to confirm the address, and Forgot password? sends a code to set a new one), or Browse as Guest. A guest can browse Community, save posts, report, block and remove posts from their feed, and use every Studio tool. Posting, commenting, messaging, liking, following and tipping need a signed-in account. Signing in later keeps what you did as a guest.
  • Your account. One account per person. Keep your password to yourself and keep access to the email address or Apple ID you sign in with, as that is how you get back into your account.
  • Sign Out. Found under Me → Settings, it ends the session on this device and keeps everything: your account, paintings, credits and the rest. Sign in again with the same email address or Apple ID to carry on.
  • Delete Account. Found under Me → Settings (shown as Clear All Data while you browse as a guest) and confirmed by typing DELETE, it deletes your account and erases your paintings, stage photos, notes, voice notes, drafts, paints, tool results, credits and credit history, profile, and your Community posts, comments, messages and settings for good, including the copies on Prma's server. If you signed in with Apple, Prma also revokes its access to your Apple ID. It needs an internet connection so the server copies can be removed first.

Keeping your iPhone or iPad locked and safe is up to you. Once data and credits are erased by Delete Account, Clear All Data or by deleting the app, neither we nor Apple can bring them back.

1.4 Tool results are estimates

Values, Mix and Compare work from your photo, the light it was taken in and the paint colors you entered, so their results are approximate. Mix and Compare always carry this line: Estimates only. Real pigments mix differently, so test on scrap paper first. We make no promise that a color, recipe or comparison will match real pigment on real paper or canvas.
