Getting started

  1. On the start screen, read the Community Terms, tick the checkbox and tap Start.
  2. Choose Continue with Apple, Continue with Email (sign in with your email address and password, or tap Create an account: enter the name other painters will see (change it any time in Me → Settings → Edit Profile), add a profile photo if you like (optional; change or remove it in Me → Settings → Edit Profile), choose a password of at least 8 characters with letters and numbers, then enter the 6-digit code Prma emails to you; check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a minute. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? to get a code and set a new one), or Browse as Guest. Guests can browse, save, report and block; sign in any time from Me → Settings to post, comment, message, like, follow and tip.
  3. Prma opens on Community. Tap Studio to create a painting, or tap Capture to photograph a stage.
  4. After each photo, Prma reads the six main colors. Add a label, a note and an optional voice note of up to 60 seconds, then save the stage.

Posting to Community

  • Start a new post from Community, then choose Painting to share the stages of a painting, Photos to pick up to 12 pictures from your library (no painting needed; the last one is the cover), or Video only to share a clip. Painting and Photos posts can also carry one video.
  • Videos come from your photo library and can be up to 60 seconds. Prma converts them to 720p on your device before uploading, so a post with a video takes a little longer to publish.
  • Tap a post's picture to see it full screen: pinch or double-tap to zoom, swipe between stages, and press and hold to save it to Photos.
  • Tap a video to play it. Press and hold while it plays, or tap ⋯ → Save video, to save it to Photos.

Credits and tips

  • You get 20 welcome credits when you create an account, enough to try the paid tools.
  • Buy credits in Me → Settings → Get credits. Payment is handled by Apple; refund requests go through Apple.
  • Tip a painter 5, 20 or 50 credits from a post. Tipped credits stay in Prma and can only be spent on tools.
  • Tips other painters send to your posts are added to your balance the next time Community refreshes.
  • Credits are kept with your Prma data on this device and are lost if you delete the app or use Delete Account or Clear All Data.

Community safety

  • Report: tap ⋯ on a post, comment or profile, or press and hold it, then choose Report. In Inbox, swipe a conversation and tap Report.
  • Block: use the same menus. Unblock in Me → Blocked members.
  • Remove from feed: hides a post from your feed at once.
  • Contact: Me → Safety & contact, or email PrmaPainting@gmail.com.

Your data

  • Sign Out: Me → Settings → Sign Out returns to the sign-in screen and keeps your account and data.
  • Delete Account: Me → Settings → Delete Account (Clear All Data for guests), then type DELETE, permanently deletes your account, your paintings, photos, recordings, credits and Community activity, including your posts, comments and messages on Prma's server. If you signed in with Apple, Prma's access to your Apple ID is revoked too. Connect to the internet first so the server copies can be removed.
