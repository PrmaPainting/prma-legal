Prma Privacy Policy

Version of November 11, 2026
Covers Prma for iPhone and iPad, bundle com.prma.apxmhu

Prma is a process journal for painters. You photograph a canvas at each stage, Prma reads the colors in that photo, and you decide whether to keep the stage to yourself or post it for other painters in Community. This page walks through the material that passes through Prma, what each piece is for, and how to remove it. The rules for using the app are in the Terms of Use.

P1. In brief

  • Using Prma takes one tap on Start. There is no email, phone number, password or real name to hand over.
  • Colors are read from your photos by code built into Prma. Your pictures, notes and voice recordings are never passed to an outside AI company.
  • Prma carries no advertising networks and no third-party analytics or tracking kits, and nothing you put in Prma is sold.
  • Anything you add can be removed on its own, and Clear All Data removes all of it in one step.

P2. What you put into Prma

Paintings and stages. A painting has a title and, if you pick one, a medium. Each stage you add to it holds a photo (from the camera or your library), the six dominant colors Prma reads from that photo as HEX values with their share of the image, a short label, a written note of up to 280 characters, and, if you want one, a spoken note of up to 60 seconds. A stage you begin but don't finish is kept as a draft so you can pick it up later.

Your paints and tool results. The Paint box holds up to 24 paints that you name and color yourself; the Mix tool builds its suggestions only from those paints. When you pay credits for a tool (Values levels, Mix, Compare check, Process sheet or the 2048px export), the result is kept so reopening it for the same photo costs nothing.

Credits. Prma keeps your current credit balance and a running history of every credit added or spent: packs bought, the 20 welcome credits added the first time you tap Start, tools paid for, and tips sent or received.

Your Community presence. The name (up to 30 characters) and bio (up to 120 characters) you set in Me, the posts, comments and direct messages you write, the posts you like and save, the painters you follow or block, the posts you remove from your feed, your report tickets, and the date you accepted the Community Terms.

App state. Whether you have tapped Start and whether you have finished the introduction screens, so Prma opens on the right screen.

P3. Where it lives and who can see it

All of the above is written to Prma's own protected storage on your iPhone or iPad: its files, its database and its preferences. Other apps cannot read that storage. It stays there until you remove it (see P9).

A stage stays private to you until you post it. Posting a stage to Community puts its photo, colors, label, note, caption, tags and medium on the Community feed and on your profile inside Prma, so post only what you are happy for other painters to see.

P4. Camera, microphone, photos and notifications

Each permission is requested through Apple's own system prompt, at the moment you first reach the feature that needs it. Any answer can be changed later in the iOS Settings app.

Permission Asked when Used for If you decline
Camera You first tap the shutter to capture a stage Photographing your canvas so its colors can be read You can still add stages from your photo library
Photos (read) You first tap Choose from library Picking an existing picture of your work You can still use the camera
Photos (add only) You first save an export or a Process sheet Writing that one image into your Photos library The image is not saved; nothing else changes
Microphone You first tap record on the stage review screen A spoken note of up to 60 seconds for that stage Stages work without voice notes
Notifications The first time the Community home screen opens Prma uses notifications to bring you updates related to the features you use, in line with how each feature works You can switch notifications on or off whenever you like in iOS Settings

Once an image is in your Photos library it is looked after by Apple's Photos app, so removing something in Prma does not remove copies you saved there.

P5. Faces in photos

This section is written to be quoted on its own as Prma's answer to App Store Review Guidelines 2.1 and 5.1.1 about facial data.

P5.1 Does Prma collect facial data?

No. Prma has no face detection, face recognition, face landmark or facial geometry code, and it never touches Face ID, the TrueDepth camera, ARKit face tracking or Vision face requests. It creates no faceprint and no other biometric identifier. The only way a face reaches Prma is inside an ordinary photo you choose, such as a stage photo where you stand beside your easel or a picture attached to a Community post. Prma handles that photo the same way as a photo of a still life.

P5.2 What a photo with a face is used for

Three things only: reading the six dominant colors of the picture; showing it where you put it (a stage, a painting, a Process sheet, an export or a Community post); and running the studio tool you open on it (Grid, Values, Mix or Compare). Prma does not look for faces, recognise who anyone is, estimate age, gender, mood or any other trait, and does not use such photos for advertising or profiling.

P5.3 Who else can see it

No outside company receives these photos, AI and analytics providers included. Other painters see a photo only after you post the stage it belongs to in Community. It reaches your Photos library only when you save an export or a Process sheet yourself.

P5.4 How long it stays and where

The photo sits in Prma's protected storage on your device for as long as the stage, painting or post that holds it exists. Deleting that stage or painting removes its photos from Prma straight away; so do Me → Settings → Clear All Data and deleting the app.

P5.5 Completeness of this section

P5.1 to P5.4 together are the whole of Prma's explanation of how images that may show a face are collected, used, disclosed, shared and retained. Prma holds no facial data beyond what is described here.

P6. Outside AI services

Prma does not use any outside AI service. Color reading, the Values, Mix and Compare tools, the Process sheet and every export are computed by Prma's own code inside the app, and no prompt, photo, note, recording or other material of yours is handed to an AI provider.

P7. Buying credits through Apple

Credit packs are consumable purchases handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Your card number, billing address and Apple ID password stay with Apple; Prma only receives Apple's confirmation that a purchase went through, then adds the credits to your balance. The pack list and credit rules are in Part 2 of the Terms of Use.

P8. Keeping Community safe

  • Text check. Before Prma saves a post, comment, message, profile, painting title, stage label, note or paint name, it compares the text with a list of abusive, hateful, sexual and spam words, including disguised spellings. Matching text is not saved or shown.
  • Reporting. Reporting a post, comment, painter or conversation hides it from you on the spot, blocks its author for you and creates a report ticket. Prma then offers an email draft to PrmaPainting@gmail.com with the ticket number, the kind of content, its ID, the author's handle, the reason you picked, any note you add and the time. The draft goes nowhere unless you send it from your mail app. Once it arrives, the Prma team looks at it within 24 hours.
  • Blocking and hiding. A blocked painter's posts, comments and messages disappear for you, and Remove from feed hides a single post. Me → Blocked members lets you undo a block.
  • Age. Community is for adults aged 18 and over.

P9. Removing your information

  • One thing at a time. A stage, painting, post, comment or paint can be deleted from its own menu. Deleting a painting takes its stages, photos, notes, voice notes and tool results with it.
  • Everything at once. In Me → Settings → Clear All Data, type DELETE to confirm. Prma then erases every painting, stage photo, note, voice note, draft, paint, tool result and export record; your credit balance and credit history; your profile; your Community posts, comments, messages, likes, saves, follows, blocks, hidden posts and report tickets; your Community Terms acceptance; and your session. Community then looks the way it does on a fresh install.
  • End Session (Me → Settings → End Session) only takes you back to the start screen. Nothing is deleted.
  • Deleting Prma from your device erases its data too. Credits lost this way cannot be brought back.

P10. Age limit

Prma is made for adults aged 18 and over and is not aimed at children. Prma does not knowingly gather information from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 is using Prma, please tell us at PrmaPainting@gmail.com.

P11. Your say over your information

Everything described in P2 can be viewed, edited or deleted inside Prma whenever you like, and any permission can be withdrawn in iOS Settings. Privacy laws in your country or state may give you further rights; write to us and we will help you use them.

P12. Updates to this page
